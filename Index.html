About Us

WHO
WE ARE

Founded in 2019, UrbanistasNW are a female-led network based in the North West of England, playing an active role shaping our cities and environment to be a more equitable and diverse place to live, work and play.

Run on a not-for-profit basis by a core team, we support women in our industry to develop their skills, grow their networks, and make their voices heard. We rely on the support and generosity of our friends and sponsors to host our events.

If you would like to collaborate with us please get in touch.
